# The VATs name

"Viewport Avatar Toolset" and "VATs" are the names of this project, used by its maintainer, Squeedledorf. The code is free software under the LGPL-2.1 and you may use, study, change and share it as that licence allows. The names are a separate matter.

## VATs is free

VATs is free and always will be. The official downloads are on this repository's [Releases page](https://github.com/squeedledorf/viewport-avatar-toolset/releases). If you paid for a copy of VATs, you were misled: please tell us where it was sold.

## Using the names

You may:

- say that your own work uses, supports or was made with VATs;
- share unmodified copies of the official releases free of charge, under the names;
- write tutorials, reviews and videos about VATs.

Please don't:

- sell VATs, or anything built from it, under the VATs names or anything confusingly close to them;
- publish a modified version under the VATs names. A fork is welcome, as the LGPL allows, but give it a name of its own and say it's based on VATs;
- suggest the project endorses your product or service.

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask.
